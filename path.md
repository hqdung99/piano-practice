# Simply Piano - Courses

## Core (purple)
- **Piano Basics** - Start your journey by learning the basic skills of piano and sight-reading
- **Essentials I** - Continue your piano journey and start playing with both hands together
- **Path chooser** (What are paths? / Soloist vs. Chords / Which path should I take?)
  - Soloist: Sheet music, sight reading, coordination and technique so you'll be playing your favorite songs and pieces
  - Chords: Learn how to play the chords of the most well-known pop, rock & jazz songs so you can accompany any song
- **Essentials II** - Expand your note range to a full octave and polish your technique
- **Essentials III** - Learn 2 octaves, flats & sharps and play your first scale
- **Skills Mastered**
  - C & F Position
  - Flats & Sharps
  - C Major Scale

- *(Optional)* **Classical I** (65%) - Start building your classical repertoire with famous classical pieces

- **Skills Mastered**

- **Intermediate II** - Let's tie up some notes - taking rhythm up a notch
- **Intermediate III** (67%) - Let's go wild. Let's extend those positions
- **Intermediate IV** (1%) - New notes and scales are waiting for you!
- *(Optional)* **Classical - Für Elise** - Learn to play the most famous piano piece of all time
  - **Skills Mastered**

- *(Optional)* **Bohemian Rhapsody** - Learn to play one of the greatest rock songs ever written!
- *(Optional)* **Taste of Bach I** - A taste of J.S. Bach, the Baroque genius - Part 1
- *(Optional)* **Taste of Bach II** - A taste of J.S. Bach, the Baroque genius - Part 2
- **Pre-Advanced I** - Learn About Key Signatures & 16th Notes
- *(Optional)* **The Entertainer** - Play one of the best known piano pieces: S. Joplin's "Ragtime" masterpiece - The Entertainer!
- *(Optional)* **Turkish March** - Learn to play one of Mozart's most famous piano pieces - Turkish March!
- **Pre-Advanced II** (1%) - Take your left hand to the next level by learning triads, Oompah and Alberti Bass!
- **Pre-Advanced III** - Learn triplets, eighth-note swing and practice position-free playing!

## Chords path (pink)
- **Pop Chords I** (3%) - Master your first 4 chords and learn to play top-charting songs
- **Chord Styling I** - Style up some soft rock patterns and start like a pro
- **Skills Mastered**
  - 5 First Chords
  - 3 Soft Rock Stylings
- **Pop Chords II** - Learn new chords so you can accompany more great hits!
- **Chord Styling II** - Learn two new style patterns to show off with
- **Skills Mastered**
  - Playing 7 Chords
  - Classic Rock Styling
  - Ballad Styling
- **Chord Styling III** - The alternating bass and a ballad style for you to use in any song
- **Pop Chords III** - Even more chords so you can play more advanced songs
- **Skills Mastered**
  - Playing 10 Chords
  - Alternating Bass Styling
  - Ballad Styling
- **Blues Chords I** - Jazz up your playing with swing, blues and rock'n roll
- **Jazz Chords I** - Learn some basic jazz and chord progressions
- **Jazz Chords II** - More jazz chords, chord progressions and new accompaniment patterns
- **Slash Chords** - Take your chord playing to the next level
- **Arpeggios I** - Learn one of the most iconic concepts in music with Adele and more!
- *(Optional)* **Someone Like You** - Feel all the feelings with Adele's iconic hit!
